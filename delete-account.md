# Delete your REMOTE DNA account

**App:** REMOTE DNA (`com.beinteck.remotedna`) — published by Solar AI Systems.

An account in REMOTE DNA is optional. It exists only so a purchased Pro licence
can follow you to a new phone. You can delete it at any time.

## Option 1 — delete it yourself, inside the app (instant)

1. Open **REMOTE DNA**.
2. Go to the **Me** tab (bottom right).
3. Scroll to **Account**.
4. Tap **Delete account permanently**, then confirm.

Your account is erased from our servers immediately.

## Option 2 — ask us by email

Send a message from the address you registered with to:

**solar.ai.systems@gmail.com**

Subject: `Delete my account`. We erase the account within **30 days** and reply
to confirm.

## What is deleted

- Your email address
- Your password (stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash — never in readable form)
- The link between your account and any Pro licence

Deletion is immediate and permanent. We keep no backup copy of the account
record.

## What is kept, and why

- **Your Pro licence stays valid.** You paid for it, so deleting the account
  does not cancel it. The licence key keeps working and can be activated again
  on any device.
- **The transaction record** (reference, amount, status, date) is retained
  because accounting and tax law require it. It is no longer linked to your
  email address after deletion.
- **Devices and codes saved on your phone** are never on our servers at all.
  Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, removes them.
- **Anonymous contributed IR codes** (if you opted in to code sharing) contain
  no personal identifier and cannot be traced back to you, so they cannot be
  individually removed.
